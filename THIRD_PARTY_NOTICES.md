# Third-party notices

## Curriculum vocabulary

The primary and middle school vocabulary collections follow Appendix 3 of China's Ministry of Education *English Curriculum Standard for Compulsory Education (2022 edition)*, available through [People's Education Press](https://www.pep.com.cn/ebook/2022yjkcbz/yy/).

The appendix lists 505 basic words at Level 2 for primary school and 1,600 at Level 3 for compulsory education. The Level 3 list includes the primary vocabulary, leaving 1,095 additional words for middle school.

The high school collection follows Appendix 2 of the *English Curriculum Standard for General High Schools (2017 edition, revised in 2020)*. The [source PDF](https://www.pep.com.cn/xw/zt/rjwy/gzkb2020/202205/P020220517522153664167.pdf) is hosted by People's Education Press.

Its full appendix contains 3,000 words, including the compulsory education foundation. One or two asterisks mark words introduced in the required and elective high school courses. The standard describes 500 additions for required courses and 1,000 for elective courses. This project includes the complete appendix, rather than only the starred entries. The generated file currently groups those additions as 499 required and 1,001 elective entries; the total remains 3,000.

The curriculum standards do not supply Chinese definitions or parts of speech, and they do not assign every primary or middle school word to a specific grade. The project does not invent grade or CEFR assignments for those lists. Pronunciations and Chinese definitions are drawn from ECDICT, with manual corrections to a small number of abbreviations, phrases, and outdated meanings.

## Middle school grammar references

The sequence of grammar topics draws on:

- Tono, Yukio, compiler. *The CEFR-J Wordlist, Version 1.6*. Tokyo University of Foreign Studies. [Download page](https://www.cefr-j.org/download.html). Accessed July 10, 2026.
- Ishii, Y. (2025). *CEFR-J Grammar Profile 2025*. [Reference PDF](https://www.cefr-j.org/PDF/sympo2025/CEFRJGP_JPTEXTBOOK202502.pdf).

CEFR-J materials are attributed to the Tono Laboratory at Tokyo University of Foreign Studies. The source website permits free research, educational, and commercial use with appropriate attribution. This project uses the level structure and grammar sequence as references; its Chinese explanations, examples, and exercises were written separately for the site.

## ECDICT

Vocabulary pronunciations and Chinese definitions are based on the [ECDICT English–Chinese dictionary](https://github.com/skywind3000/ECDICT).

The postgraduate entrance exam collection contains 4,801 entries tagged `ky`; the IELTS preparation collection contains 5,040 entries tagged `ielts`. These are ECDICT's exam-related tags. The IELTS collection is presented as a preparation resource, not an official IELTS vocabulary syllabus.
